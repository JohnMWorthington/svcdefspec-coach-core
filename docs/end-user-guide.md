# End-User Guide — ChatGPT and Claude

**SvcDefSpec Coach CORE v0.1.26**

## Who this is for

Anyone who wants help understanding, defining, or improving a service in ChatGPT or Claude.

You do not need to know USM terminology to use the Coach.

## What you need

- the public SvcDefSpec Coach CORE file;
- your service evidence;
- a new ChatGPT or Claude conversation.

Useful evidence can include:
- an SLA or service agreement;
- a service description;
- catalog entries;
- customer/user information;
- supplier or dependency information;
- performance reports;
- spreadsheets;
- workshop notes;
- interview notes;
- or simply a plain-language description of a new service.

## Start

Upload the CORE file and your evidence.

Then use this prompt:

> Use the uploaded Service Definition & Specification Coach CORE as your operating method for this conversation. Apply it to the service evidence I provide. Keep the method outside-in, preserve Current/Future/Open distinctions, and follow its phase gates.
>
> Review this material and help me understand the service it describes. Start with the current state and ask only the few questions that materially improve the definition.

## What happens next

### Phase 1 — See it + Specify it

The Coach should first help you understand the service as it exists today.

Expect:
- a simple Service Model Map;
- a Service Model Canvas;
- a working Customer/Provider boundary;
- Useful Completion;
- Facility and Support;
- evidence, inference, conflicts, and Open items;
- only a few material questions.

The Coach should not start redesigning the service yet.

### Phase 2 — Operate it

When Phase 1 is coherent, ask the Coach to move to operational detail.

It should identify only a small representative set of important customer-to-provider interactions.

For each, it may show:
- what starts the interaction;
- what the customer needs to achieve;
- the Provider’s high-level responsibility;
- important dependencies;
- agreement implications.

It should not design detailed workflows, routing, RACI, approval chains, CMDB/MIR structure, procedures, or request models.

### Phase 3 — Agree it

When you are ready to formalize the service, ask the Coach to compare the current agreement against:
- the service model already developed;
- the relevant USM Service Agreement structure.

The first step should be an Alignment & Gap Review, not immediate rewriting.

It should distinguish:
- what is already clear;
- what is scattered;
- what conflicts;
- what is ambiguous;
- what is missing but known;
- what is genuinely unknown;
- what is not needed;
- what would be a future design decision.

## Current, Future, and Open

This is one of the most important controls.

**Current** means the service or commitment exists today.

**Future** means it is proposed or agreed for later.

**Open** means it is unresolved.

The Coach should not quietly turn:
- a proposal into Current;
- a proposal into an agreed commitment;
- a conflict into a decision;
- an assumption into a fact.

## What to watch for

Good behavior:
- customer perspective first;
- useful completion before internal machinery;
- one coherent service model;
- few, material questions;
- visible assumptions;
- clear boundaries;
- no unnecessary service proliferation;
- no premature workflow design.

Warning signs:
- every team, tool, catalog item, or supplier becomes a “service”;
- the Coach starts designing workflow before the service is defined;
- missing information becomes a recommendation;
- supplier uptime is treated as proof that users can work;
- a ticket being closed is treated as proof that the service is restored;
- the Coach invents targets, priorities, roles, signatories, or commitments.

## ChatGPT notes

Use a new chat for a clean case.

Upload the CORE file and evidence.

If your ChatGPT setup can create/edit PowerPoint or Excel, you may use the public templates.

If not, structured text/tables are fine.

## Claude notes

A normal Claude chat is sufficient.

A Project is not required for the validated CORE method.

On Claude Free, larger cases may hit usage limits. Continue in the same chat after the limit resets.

## Spreadsheets

Both ChatGPT and Claude can use spreadsheet evidence where supported.

For large cases, include all material spreadsheets at the start if possible.

## When to stop

The Coach should stop once you have:
- a valid service boundary;
- a coherent Map and Canvas;
- representative interactions;
- a gap-aware Service Agreement foundation.

Detailed USM workflow/process/request design comes after this.

## FULL

The public CORE edition is enough for normal use.

## Human responsibility

The Coach is an aid, not an authority.

Review important outputs before using them for commitments, contracts, governance, or organizational decisions.
