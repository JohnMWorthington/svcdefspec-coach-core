# CORE vs FULL


## CORE


SvcDefSpec Coach CORE is the public edition.


It is intended to be methodologically correct without requiring:
- MCP;
- APIs;
- private plugins;
- connected applications;
- proprietary execution infrastructure.


CORE includes enough of the method to:
- reconstruct a service;
- model it outside-in;
- identify representative interactions;
- perform the Alignment & Gap Review;
- prepare a valid Service Agreement foundation;
- preserve the clean handoff to downstream workflow/process design.


## FULL


SvcDefSpec Coach FULL is a controlled edition available upon request.


FULL may include:
- complete controlled method/reference package;
- advanced analyzer and rule catalogs;
- execution-ready templates;
- semantic artifact mappings;
- artifact execution contracts;
- controlled USM/reference assets where redistribution rights permit;
- deployment/operator guidance;
- assurance and peer-review materials;
- governed integration support.


FULL is provided only after the applicable no-cost professional/organizational license and approval process.


## FULL + Integration


FULL + Integration adds approved MCP, API, repository, application, or other external integrations.


Integrations must follow the SvcDefSpec Integration & Extension Contract.


The governing principle is:


**External integrations provide evidence and execution.  
SvcDefSpec provides interpretation and method.  
Humans accept, approve, and commit.**


## Method parity


CORE and FULL must not contain different methodological truths.


FULL adds deployment depth and controlled capability. CORE must remain a sound implementation of the method.


## Distribution


CORE may be distributed through the official public repository/release channel.


FULL must be distributed separately through a private repository, private release, or controlled package. It must not be hidden in a branch of the public repository.
