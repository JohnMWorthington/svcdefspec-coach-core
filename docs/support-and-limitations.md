# Support and Limitations


## AI limitations


The Coach can:
- extract;
- classify;
- compare;
- infer cautiously;
- identify contradictions;
- structure service models;
- prepare draft agreement material.


It can still be wrong.


Human review is required for material decisions.


## No automatic authority


The Coach does not have authority to:
- accept a service definition on behalf of a Customer or Provider;
- approve commitments;
- authorize funding;
- sign agreements;
- make legal determinations;
- certify USM conformance;
- perform a formal Dual Lens diagnosis.


## Host limitations


Capabilities vary by platform and plan.


Some hosts can create/edit PPTX/XLSX/DOCX artifacts; others may only produce structured text. The method should still work.


Usage limits, context limits, file-size limits, and spreadsheet handling may vary by host.


## Data responsibility


Do not provide confidential, personal, regulated, or sensitive data without appropriate authority and safeguards.


The user/deployer is responsible for deciding whether data may be sent to the chosen AI platform.


## Support


The public CORE release is provided without a commitment to ongoing support, maintenance, training, troubleshooting, updates, or future availability.


Professional peer review, FULL access, or approved integration/deployment support may be available separately.
